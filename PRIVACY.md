# Privacy Policy

Take Home, built by Base16 Labs

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Take Home does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

- **No accounts.** You don't sign in or create an account.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
- **No network requests.** All tax calculations run on your device. The app only opens a web page if you tap one of the links in Settings.
- **On-device storage only.** Your chosen country, the pay and contribution figures you enter, and your appearance setting are saved on your device so the app reopens where you left off. They never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Take Home gives estimates, not tax advice.
